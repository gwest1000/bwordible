Guide

How MannaGrams works

Guess today’s 4, 5, or 6 letter Bible word. Type a word, then press Enter to see how close you are.

  • You get two more guesses than the number of letters.
  • Plural words are allowed.
✓A CorrectRight letter, right place
↔A CloseRight letter, wrong place
×A Not presentThe letter isn’t in the word
About the daily puzzle

Answers include people, places, books, objects, themes, and familiar words used in the Bible. A new puzzle appears every day at midnight Pacific time, and the answer order reshuffles annually. A win streak counts consecutive wins when you play; skipped days do not break it. A play streak counts consecutive calendar days on which you complete the puzzle.
